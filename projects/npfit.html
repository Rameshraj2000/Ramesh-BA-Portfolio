NPfIT UK Healthcare IT Transformation

A programme-level case study focused on stakeholder and process analysis, requirements challenges, change management, governance and a phased implementation roadmap.

Case-study scope

This case study examines delivery challenges associated with the UK's National Programme for IT in the NHS (NPfIT), focusing on business-analysis themes: requirements, stakeholder engagement, change management and governance.

Project type: Research and analysis case study. This is not a claim of personal involvement in the NPfIT programme or a live client engagement.

Analysis approach

  1. Frame the programme context and identify the delivery and adoption challenges being examined.
  2. Map relevant stakeholder groups, their needs, influence and potential concerns.
  3. Compare the current-state challenges with a proposed future-state approach to stakeholder engagement and delivery governance.
  4. Analyse change impacts and dependencies that could affect implementation and adoption.
  5. Translate the analysis into a phased roadmap with priorities and decision points.

Business-analysis outputs

  • Root-cause analysis of delivery challenges
  • Stakeholder analysis and engagement considerations
  • AS-IS / TO-BE process comparisons
  • Change-impact assessment
  • Phased implementation roadmap

How I would apply the learning

For a complex transformation, I would establish clear decision rights, document assumptions and dependencies, maintain a visible change-impact log, and validate requirements with the people affected by the change. I would also break implementation into manageable phases with explicit ownership, readiness checks and feedback loops.

The case is useful for discussing how BA work can support alignment across stakeholders, requirements and governance in a complex programme. The roadmap is an analytical proposal, not evidence of outcomes achieved by the historical programme.
